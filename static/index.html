Tips
Need captions, a dub or a re-said line? Switch to Voice at the top left, or press Voice it after a video is cleaned. Real text in the scene (signs, shirts, labels) getting removed? Draw a Text zone over where the captions sit. Leftover outlines? Raise Edge padding. Missed faint text? Raise Sensitivity.
